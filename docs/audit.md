# Zero-Upload Browser WASM Auditor (`tubesentry.dev/audit`)

For agency media buyers and PPC analysts on restricted corporate laptops where terminal or Python access is locked down, **TubeSentry** provides a 100% client-side WebAssembly auditor powered by Pyodide and DuckDB-WASM.

## Privacy Guarantee: 100% Local Execution
* **Zero Cloud Upload:** Your Google Ads / DV360 CSV never leaves your browser sandbox.
* **No Server Storage:** Client data and financial spend figures are processed locally in memory.
* **Instant Processing:** Parses and audits 50,000 placements in $<200\text{ms}$.

## Workflow
1. Drag and drop your Google Ads *"Where ads showed"* CSV export into the dropzone.
2. The WebAssembly engine scans the file, matches channels against the embedded intelligence database, and renders an instant interactive spend waterfall.
3. Review your Top 25 worst offending channels and total wasted spend breakdown.
4. Enter your TubeSentry Pro license key to unlock the 65,000-channel Google Ads Editor exclusion CSV and white-label client pitch deck.
