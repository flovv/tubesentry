# Supported Waste Vectors

TubeSentry classifies placement waste across 6 discrete algorithmic bleed vectors.

---

## 1. Made-for-Kids & Toddler Content (`MADE_FOR_KIDS`)
* **Typical Channels:** CoComelon, Bebefinn, ChuChu TV, Baby Shark, nursery rhymes, sensory baby animations.
* **Why it Wastes Money:** Toddlers tapping on parent phones or tablets without purchase intent. Generates high accidental CTR, near-instant bounce rates, and exposes advertisers to legal COPPA / GDPR-K scrutiny.
* **Sub-categories:** `YOUNG_KIDS_TODDLER` (<10 / nursery rhymes) vs. `GENERAL_KIDS_CARTOON`.

## 2. Passive Audio & Sleep Music (`PASSIVE_AUDIO_MUSIC`)
* **Typical Channels:** 24/7 Lofi Hip Hop radio, sleep white noise, rain sounds, meditation loops, binaural study beats.
* **Why it Wastes Money:** Users play audio in background tabs, while sleeping, or with the monitor off. Visual ads receive 0% view attention.

## 3. Underage Minor Gaming Drains (`LOW_AGE_GAMING`)
* **Typical Channels:** Roblox gameplay roleplays, Minecraft mod showcases, Brawl Stars, Skibidi Toilet meme compilations.
* **Why it Wastes Money:** Audiences skew heavily under 16. Incompatible with adult direct-response or B2B campaigns.

## 4. Foreign Script Geo-Leakage (`FOREIGN_SCRIPT_LEAK`)
* **Typical Channels:** Hindi news networks, Cyrillic / Russian animation channels, Arabic talk shows, Thai / Vietnamese broadcast streams.
* **Why it Wastes Money:** Language and geographic misalignment in localized European or North American campaigns.

## 5. AI Slop & Synthetic Content Farms (`AI_SLOP_CLICK_FARM`)
* **Typical Channels:** Split-screen Subway Surfers + synthetic AI voiceover facts, Reddit text-to-speech farms.
* **Why it Wastes Money:** Low-engagement content created for bot click arbitrage and automated ad monetization.

## 6. Performance Sinkholes (`PERFORMANCE_SINKHOLE`)
* **Characteristics:** High spend (>3x target CPA) with zero attributed conversions and poor view completion rates.
